# CRM Analytics Dashboard Wireframe

## Executive KPIs

Active customers, current MRR, churn rate, retention rate, average LTV, at-risk accounts, and at-risk MRR.

## Customer Health Distribution

Stacked bar or donut showing Healthy, Watch, At Risk, and Critical accounts with MRR weighting.

## Churn Risk Queue

Table sorted by health score and MRR with account, segment, region, plan tier, risk driver, and recommended action.

## Retention Cohort Heatmap

Rows are signup cohorts. Columns are months since signup. Color is retention rate.

## LTV by Segment

Bar chart by segment and plan tier using gross margin and churn assumptions.

## Support Impact on Churn

Scatter or grouped bar showing escalations, resolution rate, health score, and churn rate.

## Expansion Opportunities

List accounts with strong health, current MRR, and weighted open pipeline.

## Recommended Actions

Text table or actions list from the Customer 360 risk-driver logic.
